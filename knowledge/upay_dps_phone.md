MTB Islamic DPS on upay (condensed from knowledge/upay_dps.md, the official page text)
What: Shariah-compliant Deposit Pension Scheme (monthly savings) from Mutual Trust Bank (MTB), opened and managed in the upay app, fully digital. Monthly instalments are auto-deducted from the customer's upay account. At maturity principal + profit (minus source tax and excise duty) are credited to the upay account; withdrawing the maturity amount costs nothing extra.
Plans: 24, 36 or 48 months, with a monthly deposit of 200, 500, 1,000 or 2,000 Taka (any combination). Several DPS can be open at the same time.
Who can open: any active, full-profile upay customer with valid ID and an active mobile number.
Charges: no paperwork, no processing fee, no hidden charges from upay or MTB.
Instalments: keep enough balance in the upay account; SMS reminder 1 day before; if the balance is too low the debit fails and upay retries for up to 2 days; if still unpaid the month is marked missed and earns no profit.
Early closing: not possible in the first 3 months; after 3 months allowed with applicable charges (standard cash-out rates). Profit if closed early: under 12 months none; from 12 to under 24 months the 12-month slab rate; from 24 to under 36 the 24-month slab rate; from 36 to under 48 the 36-month slab rate (remaining days at the savings product rate).
Tax on profit: 10% if e-TIN proof is given to MTB (can be submitted any time under 'Savings' in the upay app), otherwise 15%. An e-TIN can be obtained from the NBR website.
Nominee: can be changed any time. The customer is responsible for correct nominee details; wrong details can affect settlement or claims and upay is not liable.
Terms can be changed by MTB and upay without prior notice.
NOT stated anywhere: profit rate or percentage, maturity amount, other products, anything about a customer's own account.
